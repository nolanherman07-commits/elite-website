What it means

Jose's take

For buyers

Homes are taking more than two months to sell and there are over four months of supply. That means more choices and more room to negotiate on price, repairs and closing costs.

For sellers

Prices are holding steady, but buyers are patient. Homes priced right from day one and marketed well are the ones that sell fastest.

City vs. coast

Moving a little north into Pender County or south into Brunswick County can get you more home or more land for the money, still close to the beach.
