Why people love Carolina Beach

Carolina Beach is on Pleasure Island, south of Wilmington, with a classic boardwalk, a fishing pier and Carolina Beach State Park. It offers a more relaxed beach-town feel, from condos to raised beach homes.

  • Boardwalk, pier and summer events
  • Beach homes, condos and investment properties
  • Carolina Beach State Park
  • Easy drive to Wilmington
