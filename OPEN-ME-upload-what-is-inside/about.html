My story

Real estate runs in my family. My grandpa was an investor. I emailed brokers out of high school until one said yes.

Growing up, I had a front-row seat to how property can change a family's future. Right out of high school I started emailing local brokers asking for a chance to learn. Most didn't answer. One did, and that email turned into an internship, and that internship turned into a career. Two years later, I've helped clients buy and sell over $10 million in homes, and every one of my listings has sold.
